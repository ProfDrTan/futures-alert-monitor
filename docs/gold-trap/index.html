The Gold Trap

One Trade, One Week, and the Rules That Stop You Losing It All

Prof. Dr. Tan Teik Kheong, Adjunct Professor, Stanford University · a book in progress, written live

A live futures trade, told hour by hour, with an AI co-pilot that kept score of its own calls. Almost every trading book is written in hindsight by a winner. This one is written as it happened: the entries, the averaging down, the stress, a drawdown past $40,000, and every call the AI got right or wrong, timestamped and graded. Each moment becomes a rule, and each rule becomes a tool you can use before your next order.

moment → mistake → rule → tool

The four parts

Part I · The Week

The trade, day by day, from the missed 09:00 rally on 20 Oct 2026 to the close of the position. Each chapter opens with a timestamp and ends with the P/L.

Part II · The Rules

Eight rules, each tied to the exact moment it would have changed, with the dollar difference.

Part III · The Co-Pilot

Trading with an AI: the scorecard, what it got right (timing) and wrong (direction, stale data, the wrong multiplier), and how to keep your own judgement.

Part IV · The Toolkit

Pre-trade checklist, position-size calculator, journal template and the 09:00 SGT volatility research.
